\section{Completion Tests and Falsifiability}
\label{sec:falsifiability}

A falsifiable prediction requires a specified physical model. The current formal results identify concrete tests of candidate updates rather than a completed exclusion of all alternative particle content or gravity theories.

\begin{enumerate}
 \item \textbf{Fermion spectrum.} Derive the full momentum-dependent two-sector spectrum, multiplicities and chirality. Prove that no unwanted zeros remain away from the origin, rather than checking only corners or a numerical grid.
 \item \textbf{Discrete time and locality.} Construct an exactly unitary update with the required causal stencil, or bound the propagation tails of an exponential update. Prove a bandwidth condition that excludes phase aliasing across the entire spectrum.
 \item \textbf{Shared propagation response.} Derive common low-energy light cones and controlled finite-spacing deviations for fermion, gauge and scalar sectors. Unit link speed alone is insufficient.
 \item \textbf{Gauge and scalar response.} Extend the constant Abelian triangle result to nonconstant and non-Abelian fields. Derive kinetic normalizations, representations and coupling coefficients. Test the scalar/fermion mass map rather than equating detuning with a linear Yukawa mass by definition.
 \item \textbf{Dynamical observables.} Compute scattering, vertex response and vacuum polarization from the completed update and its species content. The current cross-section comparison and assigned Schwinger factor are not those calculations.
 \item \textbf{Gravity.} Construct the metric response, its continuum equations and physical spectrum. Only then test a stiffness normalization, equivalence principle or cosmological prediction.
\end{enumerate}

The present proofs do not exclude a fourth generation, supersymmetry, higher spins, higher-dimensional effective interactions or evolving dark energy. Such exclusions would require representation, dynamics and observable theorems not currently established. If a completed model predicts them, its assumptions and quantitative tests should be stated at that stage.
