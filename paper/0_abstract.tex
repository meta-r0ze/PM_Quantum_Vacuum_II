\begin{abstract}
Persistence Mechanics proposes an operational action that prices the maintenance of a system's required distinctions. We study conditional mathematical routes from this cost model to field dynamics on the $E_8$ substrate of Paper I. Lean verifies finite survival-weight concentration, phase-covariant transport, a first-order dispersion/Clifford equivalence, the sign conditions for a nonzero stable scalar minimum, and finite-dimensional reconstruction of unitary phase evolution under detailed balance and injectivity assumptions.

The concrete lattice results include the isotropic area tensor of the minimal $D_4$ triangle loops, a controlled Abelian constant-field continuum limit, two-sector chiral zero-mode conditions, and a detuning mass relation. These results distinguish exact lattice identities from continuum approximations. Further checked constructions connect actual glue-class reflection events to primitive cost
and normalized trace scores. A specified phase-sensitive readout produces the cosine repair
cost, and a unitary-eigenbasis link expansion has a controlled remainder. The selected
fermion Hamiltonian has an exact signed spectrum and a local unitary implementation preserving
its known vacuum modes. Physical carrier/representation selection, finite-tick spectral
exhaustion, the microscopic readout and repair law, non-Abelian continuum response, physical
normalization and emergent gravity remain to be established. Finite capacity alone does not prove exact elimination of higher-dimensional operators, a Born-rule derivation, or a unique Standard Model and Einstein--Hilbert action. The paper states the additional assumptions and remaining tests needed for those stronger conclusions.
\end{abstract}
