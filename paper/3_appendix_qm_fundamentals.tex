\section{Quantum Reconstruction and the Remaining Measurement Problem}
\label{app:quantum_fundamentals}

The formal results separate deterministic evolution from statistical history selection. A bijection of a finite configuration space permutes arbitrary complex amplitudes and preserves their squared norm (\nolinkurl{Reconstruction.bijective_update_unitary}). This construction does not select amplitudes or a measurement rule.

For a statistical transfer matrix $T$ and positive weights $\pi$, detailed balance
$\pi_xT_{xy}=\pi_yT_{yx}$ makes
\begin{equation}
 S_{xy}=\sqrt{\pi_x}\,T_{xy}/\sqrt{\pi_y}
\end{equation}
symmetric. If $S$ is also injective, $S^2$ is positive definite. Its positive eigenvalues define real logarithmic energies, and spectral phase evolution $c_i(t)=e^{-iE_it}c_i(0)$ preserves $\sum_i|c_i|^2$. Lean verifies these finite-dimensional steps. Detailed balance, injectivity and the relation between this transfer model and the microscopic update are premises. A full quantum-field reconstruction, causal locality and a measurement interpretation do not follow from the spectral calculation alone.

\subsection{Survival Weights Are Not Yet a Born-Rule Derivation}
For nonzero amplitudes, assigning $S_k=-\log|c_k|^2+S_{\rm base}$ gives weights proportional to $|c_k|^{2\beta}$. At $\beta=1$ their normalization reproduces $|c_k|^2$. This is an algebraic conditional statement: it puts the squared amplitudes into the assigned action and does not derive that assignment or the value of $\beta$. No Born-rule theorem is currently claimed from this argument.

\subsection{Correlations and Projection}
The orthogonal-sector decomposition does not, by itself, construct an entangled state, Bell correlations or a no-signalling measurement theory. Equal internal coordinates do not remove a nonzero spacetime contribution to an orthogonal distance formula. An instantaneous internal update is therefore not inferred merely from a shared internal label. A physical account requires a joint state space, local observables and their causal evolution.

\subsection{Measurement and Basis Selection}
\label{subsec:measurement}
A projection rule $P_k\psi/\sqrt{\langle\psi,P_k\psi\rangle}$ can be stated once an observable, probability law and apparatus interaction are supplied. They are not selected by the current node-capacity count. No proof presently connects measurement to a unique capacity-exhaustion threshold, an entropy increment $\log2$, or a preferred basis. These remain questions for a completed microscopic interaction model, rather than conclusions of the finite reconstruction theorem.
