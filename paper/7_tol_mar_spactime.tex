\section{Toll and Margin: The Gravitational Proposal}
\label{sec:toll_margin}

The proposed interpretation associates metric response with maintaining causal connections (Toll), and a volume term with a baseline resolution cost (Margin). The current Lean package does not derive metric dynamics, an Einstein equation, a spin-two spectrum, or a cosmological constant from the substrate. This section states the target and the additional work it requires.

\subsection{Capacity and Metric Response}
A proposed available-capacity ledger is
\begin{equation}
 C_{\rm available}(x)=\nu-\mathbf K(x).
\end{equation}
This accounting identity does not specify a metric. In particular, the suggested weak-field relations
\begin{equation}
 g_{00}\simeq-\left(1-\frac{\mathbf K}{\nu}\right),\qquad
 \Phi\simeq-\frac{\mathbf K}{2\nu}
\end{equation}
require a derived propagation/response law and the identification $g_{00}\simeq-(1+2\Phi)$. Neither follows from the deficit alone. A refractive-index interpretation also requires its own transport equation.

Using the same symbol $\mathbf K$ in inertial and gravitational ledgers is not a proof of equal physical masses. A completion must compute both responses from the same maintained state and establish their normalization and coupling to probes.

\subsection{The Einstein--Hilbert Target}
The candidate continuum action is
\begin{equation}
 S_{\rm gravity}=\int d^4x\sqrt{-g}\,\frac{M_P^2}{2}(R-2\Lambda).
\end{equation}
Uniqueness results for a four-dimensional, local, coordinate-invariant metric theory with the specified second-order field-equation assumptions \cite{lovelock_einstein_1971} motivate this target. Those assumptions must first be obtained from the lattice. A nearest-neighbour microscopic stencil alone does not prove exact exclusion of higher continuum derivatives or curvature terms.

A proof would need an explicit geometric state and update, a metric observable, a controlled continuum limit, its symmetry identities and propagating-mode spectrum. No ghost-free or pure spin-two theorem is presently supplied, even at linear order. The bare stiffness, the Einstein--Hilbert normalization and a physical equivalence-principle statement remain open.

\subsection{The Volume Term}
Identifying a baseline resolution cost with $\rho_{\rm vac}=M_P^2\Lambda$ is a proposed matching condition. The Margin pillar does not yet prove that the effective vacuum contribution is static or that all additional scalar dynamics are forbidden. Consequently no exclusion of dynamical dark energy is inferred here. A completed model must derive the volume response and its conservation law alongside the metric dynamics.
